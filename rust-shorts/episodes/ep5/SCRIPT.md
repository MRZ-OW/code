# Ep. 5: "Naked Privilege"

**Logline.** The patrol helicopter scans the Naked and lets him go: it only hunts geared players. He tests the rule one clothing piece at a time and trolls the heli while it shreds everyone else. Then he picks up one AK. After the explosion, the heli scans him again and moves on.

- **Length:** 24.0 s = 12 bars at 120 BPM.
- **Outcome:** greed is punished. The opening scan returns as the closing beat.
- **Label:** none. The premise reveals itself.
- **Insider hooks:** the patrol heli ignores nakeds and targets players wearing about 3+ pieces of clothing or holding a gun (community lore); its minigun, rockets and searchlight; player remains sacks.

**The setting:** a grassy plain near a monument, with big grey oil storage tanks and a radio tower in the hazy distance, at daytime. **The patrol heli:** a big dark-olive military helicopter with a blurred main rotor, a tail rotor, a nose searchlight, side rocket pods and a chin minigun. The Naked is u = 36, feet at y 1360.
