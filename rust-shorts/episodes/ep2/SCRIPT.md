# Ep. 2: "1234"

**Logline.** The Naked tries to guess a base's code lock, and every wrong guess electrocutes him harder. The owner walks up and types 1234. When the Naked tries 1234 himself, the owner has quietly changed it from inside. The mega-zap blasts him away, and the owner takes his rock too.

- **Length:** 24.0 s = 12 bars at 120 BPM.
- **Outcome:** total humiliation.
- **Label:** none. The lock's digits are the only lettering: "0000", "6969", "1234".
- **Insider hooks:** the code lock zaps you for wrong guesses, harder each time; 1234 and 6969 are among the most-guessed codes; the armoured door's hatch; stealing a naked's rock.

**Camera setups**

- **W (wide):** dusk, outside a stone base. A stone wall face with an armoured door (dark riveted steel with a horizontal viewing hatch) at x 400–680, y 760–1290. The code lock is to the right of the door at x 720, y 1000. Ground at y 1300. The Naked is u = 34.
- **K (keypad close-up):** the code lock fills the upper safe box, about 560×700 px centred at (468, 760). Its display shows a red/green LED and four digits; below are 3×4 cream keys. A finger enters from the lower right: the Naked's bare finger, or the Chad's brown leather glove.
- **R (reaction close-up):** the Naked from the chest up, u = 70, head at about y 800.
