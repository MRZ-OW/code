# Ep. 3: "Eoka"

**Logline.** In a standoff, the Naked's eoka pistol won't fire. The Chad is so unimpressed that he eats beans and sleeps through a whole day and night. The Naked finally gives up and tosses it, and it fires. Then it fires first try. Then it fires again, mid-hug, into his own foot.

- **Length:** 28.0 s = 14 bars at 120 BPM.
- **Outcome:** a win, ruined by the eoka itself.
- **Label:** "Eoka" (0.0–2.5 s).
- **Insider hooks:** the eoka really does need repeated strikes and may never fire ("a very cheap, very ineffective, and very unreliable pistol"); eoka faith; a can of beans; a chicken.

**Camera setups**

- **2S:** profile two-shot in a meadow with a gravel road, a rusty barrel and distant power lines. The Naked is on the left (x 260, side view facing right, u = 36, feet at y 1330); the Chad is on the right (x 790, side view facing left).
- **N:** the Naked from the waist up, u = 64.
- **C:** close-up on the Chad's mask.

**The eoka (from the icon):** a bent wooden stick with a taped metal pipe barrel. To fire it, he strikes its side with his rock: sparks, and usually nothing.
