# Ep. 1: "Friendly?"

**Logline.** A naked meets a geared player. They become friends by a campfire. The naked could steal the sleeping man's AK but leaves him a flower instead, and gets shot in the back for it. He respawns, and here comes the next stranger.

- **Length:** 28.0 s = 14 bars at 120 BPM. Loops: the last frame recreates frame 1.
- **Outcome:** betrayal. Kindness loses, which is very Rust.
- **Label:** "Friendly?" (0.0–2.6 s, top of the safe box).
- **Insider hooks:** "Friendly?" as the universal opener, the surrender gesture, a can of beans, the AK, respawning on the beach.

Staging units: the wide two-shots use u = 36 with feet at y 1330, so the eyes sit at about y 940. Everything important stays inside x 48–888, y 288–1248.
