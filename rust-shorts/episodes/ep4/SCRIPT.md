# Ep. 4: "Hit the X"

**Logline.** The red X on a tree keeps hopping away from the Naked's rock. He chases it around the trunk and onto a boar's backside. It jumps onto the Chad's facemask, CLANG, and then onto the Naked's own forehead. The Chad pulls out a rock of his own. Loop.

- **Length:** 20.0 s = 10 bars at 120 BPM. Loops: the last shot recreates the first.
- **Outcome:** irony, a bonk.
- **Label:** "Hit the X" (0.0–2.5 s). It explains the mechanic to non-players.
- **Insider hooks:** hitting a tree makes a red X appear, and hitting the X gives bonus wood; boars; a fully geared player carrying a rock.
- **Continuity:** the Naked wears a small plaster cross on his forehead from frame 1, a hint that he has done this before.

**Camera setup:** a midday pine forest. One big pine trunk (about 180 px wide) at x 380, ground at y 1380, background pines in two paler depth layers. The Naked is u = 40.
