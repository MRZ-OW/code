# Rock Bottom: series bible

A wordless, hand-painted cartoon series of YouTube Shorts about the survival game **Rust**. Every episode follows **the Naked**: a fresh spawn in Rust's Twitch-purple underwear with nothing but a rock, an open heart and terrible luck. Everyone else on the server is better armed.

*Unofficial fan animation, not affiliated with Facepunch Studios. Everything is painted in code; no game art, UI, logos, sounds or music are used.*

Research behind every choice below: [research/](research/) (Shorts craft, the Rust short-form landscape, Rust culture and visuals).

## Why this series, why now

- **The gap is real.** No one runs a recurring, original, wordless or cute Rust cartoon. Fan cartoons peaked at about 305K views years ago. Animation that works today re-enacts streamer audio (BurntPeanutAnimated). Facepunch licensed chibi Youtooz figures (Survivor, Hazmat), so cute Rust has precedent, but nobody has animated it ([landscape](research/rust_shorts_landscape.md) §4).
- **Wordless travels.** Russian-language Rust skits regularly reach 5–16M views, and the top comment on one wordless POV reads: "I've never been in a place where I understood nothing but understood everything at the same time."
- **The game's reputation is the joke.** Betrayal, misfires, overkill, door campers, absurd hours. Non-players get these from the visuals alone.
- **Originality is rewarded.** Since 2 Oct 2026 YouTube has been cutting reach for lightly edited reuploads and favouring original work.

## The cast

The rock is the Naked's one possession, and he treats it like a friend. Episodes vary premise **and** outcome (a win, a humiliation, a betrayal, an irony). Under YouTube's Partner Program rules, every episode must stand alone and must not end the same way every time.

## Rules for every episode

1. **Length 18–30 s.** That's the best-performing range for single-gag skits (median views peak at 11–30 s).
2. **Cold open.** Frame 1 already shows the Naked, what he wants and the threat, already in motion. No logo, title card or fade-in.
3. **A new beat every 2–4 s.** Use a rule-of-three escalation, then the twist. Restate the goal just before the payoff.
4. **Punchline in the last 1–2 s, then cut.** No outro or end card. Loop where the story allows: respawning on the beach is a built-in loop.
5. **The picture carries the joke.** Every episode must work with the sound off. Sound adds the timing and the final punch.
6. **Text:** one optional painted label of 1–3 words (a "premise tag") in the top of the safe box for the first ~2.5 s. Code-lock digits are the only other lettering. Pictogram speech bubbles (a heart, a "?", a smiley) are allowed; words in bubbles are not.
7. **Vertical safe box (1080×1920):** faces, props, the punchline and text go inside x 48–888, y 288–1248 (Google's Shorts safe-zone template). Put eyes around y 600–950. Bodies and motion can go down to about y 1450. Treat the bottom ~470 px, and the right ~190 px in the lower half, as background only.
8. **Bloodless slapstick.** Use smoke puffs, stars, soot and squash, never blood. Gun hits are implied (a flash, a cut, a respawn). The rock's red smear stays, as it's the item's look. Explosions and zaps stay under 3 flashes per second.
9. **Facepunch fan-content rules:** never call it official; no game logos, UI, icons, sounds or music; everything is redrawn.

## Look and sound

- **Look:** p5.brush watercolour washes with boiling ink outlines, painted on cream paper and boiling 12 times a second. Characters have strong outlines and contrast, and backgrounds stay quieter. Rust's palette is muted greens, sand, concrete grey and rusty orange, with warm firelight and cold blue nights.
- **Sound:** an original synthesized score for each episode, on a 120 BPM grid (one bar = 2 s, so cuts land on bars or half bars). Original synthesized sound effects match the game's signature sounds: rock thock, keypad beeps, the code-lock zap, eoka scrape-click-BANG, the AK, rotor thump, C4 beeps. Master to about −14 LUFS with true peak ≤ −1 dBTP, and never open quietly.

## Upload kit (for each episode)

- **Title:** the premise first, then the episode number, 40–60 characters, e.g. `Friendly? | Rock Bottom Ep. 1 (Rust cartoon)`.
- **Hashtags (3–5):** `#rustgame #playrust #animation #shorts`. Not `#rust`, which is also the programming language.
- **Description:** `Unofficial fan animation, not affiliated with Facepunch Studios.`
- **Audience:** not made for kids (Rust is rated M; the humour is gaming humour).
- **Playlist:** "Rock Bottom: Season 1", non-serial, so it can become a Shorts series. Link each Short to the next one as its related video.
- **Cadence:** 2–3 a week on fixed days, with a buffer of finished episodes.

## Idea bank (premise → payoff), ranked by fit

These are distilled from the research. ★ marks the five produced first.

1. ★ **Friendly?** The Naked and the Chad bond by a campfire. He spares the Chad's AK and leaves him a flower. Shot in the back, respawn, "Friendly?" again (loop).
2. ★ **1234.** Code-lock guesses electrocute him, more each time. The owner walks up and types 1234, then quietly changes it from inside. The Naked's final 1234 nearly fries him. The Chad takes his rock too.
3. ★ **Eoka.** The eoka won't fire. The Chad waits a whole day, eats beans and naps. The eoka fires when tossed away, then fires first try, then fires again while being hugged (into the Naked's foot).
4. ★ **Hit the X.** The tree's red X keeps jumping away from his rock. It hops onto a boar, then onto the Chad's facemask: CLANG. Then onto the Naked's own forehead. Loop.
5. ★ **Naked privilege.** The patrol heli ignores nakeds. He tests the 3-clothing rule, trolls the heli while geared players get shredded, then picks up one AK.
6. **Beancan stare-down.** A dud beancan; the Naked and a raider wait, share snacks, then he pokes it.
7. **Door camper.** Every exit from his 1×1 gets him shot. Escalating disguises, until the camper is replaced by a bear.
8. **Airdrop.** Purple smoke, a server-wide stampede, the crate lands on him, everyone loots it off his flattened body, then a second drop.
9. **Twig base.** A proud first 2×2, raided by another naked with one rock in one hit.
10. **Solo at 3 AM.** His tiny campfire sets off a clan's overkill: rockets, a mortar, a minicopter. Then the fire goes out.
11. **Bags destroyed.** Rust+ phone alerts escalate while he's in the bath.
12. **Outpost.** He draws his rock in the safe zone and the turrets turn; he hides it behind his back and whistles, again and again.
13. **Big Wheel.** He bets his only scrap on 20×. The wheel ticks past 20 and lands on 1.
14. **Chicken whisperer.** A pet chicken needs head pats; it ends up as his bodyguard.
15. **Ugly base, loaded base.** Raiders laugh past his crooked hut; inside, it's a glittering vault.
16. **Wipe day.** A hundred nakeds sprint past him on the beach. He picks a flower.
17. **Bagged in.** A stranger's sleeping bag respawns him inside a locked clan base.
18. **Worst weapons, ranked.** Five slapstick fails; #1 is the rock, his best friend.
