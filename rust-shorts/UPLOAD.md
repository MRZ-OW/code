# Rock Bottom: upload kit

Five vertical shorts (1080 × 1920, 24 fps, stereo, −14 LUFS). Upload them as Shorts in this order, two or three a week
on fixed days, into a playlist called **Rock Bottom: Season 1**, and set each one's related video to the next episode.

Every upload uses:

- **Audience:** not made for kids (Rust is rated M; this is gaming humour).
- **Hashtags:** `#rustgame #playrust #animation #shorts` (not `#rust`, which is the programming language).
- **Description footer:** `Unofficial fan animation, not affiliated with Facepunch Studios.`
- **No game footage, logos, UI or sounds** are used: everything is drawn and the music and effects are synthesized.
