# Rust: comedy and visual research brief (game state as of 9 Oct 2026)

**How to read this.**
- **Comedy tags:** [UNRELIABLE] [LOUD] [SLOW] [BOOM] mark items with built-in physical comedy.
- **"Unverified":** widely known from play, but I found no solid source.
- **Where item looks come from:** I checked them directly against Facepunch's official icons (`https://files.facepunch.com/rust/item/<shortname>_512.png`, e.g. [rifle.ak](https://files.facepunch.com/rust/item/rifle.ak_512.png)).
- **Sources I couldn't reach:** Reddit (login wall), Rust Labs and Fandom (Cloudflare). Community lore is therefore cited through press, YouTube or Steam, or flagged.

## 1. Core loop and rules that create comedy

**Spawn**
- You start naked on a random beach with a Rock and a Torch ([KYM](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/subcultures/rust)).
- Nudity has been *pixelated* by default since Mar 2024 ([Lighting the Way](https://rust.facepunch.com/news/lighting-the-way)).
- New players get a private Tutorial Island with an NPC guide. Anyone with 50+ deaths has to start it manually (same post).
- Your looks are assigned, not chosen. The June 2026 player-model rebuild kept the race and gender seeds ([Built Different](https://rust.facepunch.com/news/built-different)).

**Gathering**
- Hit a tree and a red **X** appears on the trunk. Hitting the X gives bonus wood ([Rustafied](https://www.rustafied.com/updates/2017/11/16/timmmberrr)).
- Ore nodes show a glowing spot that jumps to a new place after each hit.
- The Rock does 10 damage, doubled on headshots ([wiki](https://wiki.facepunch.com/rust/item/rock)).

**Building**
- The Building Plan places flimsy **twig** pieces.
- The Hammer upgrades each piece: wood (200) → stone (300) → sheet metal (200 metal fragments) → armoured (25 high-quality metal) ([costs](https://www.doomhosting.com/tools/rust-upkeep-calculator)).
- Every wall has a weak inner "soft side" that pickaxes chew through. A wall placed backwards is effectively a door ([guide](https://xgamingserver.com/blog/rust-raiding-guide/)).
- Defence vocabulary ([wiki](https://wiki.facepunch.com/rust/Building-terminology)):
  - **Airlock:** two doors in a row.
  - **Honeycomb:** extra layers of wall.

**Tool Cupboard (TC)** ([wiki](https://wiki.facepunch.com/rust/the_tool_cupboard))
- It's a wardrobe that costs 1,000 wood.
- Authorised players see "BUILDING PRIVILEGE". Strangers get a red outline and "BUILDING BLOCKED", though they can still place twig floors and ladders.
- It holds the upkeep resources. When it runs empty, the base decays from the outside in:

  | Tier | Time to fully decay |
  |---|---|
  | Twig | 1 h |
  | Wood | 3 h |
  | Stone | 5 h |
  | Metal | 8 h |
  | Armoured | 12 h |

- Since Sep 2026, upkeep is taxed per head ([Breach and Clear](https://rust.facepunch.com/news/breach-and-clear)). The first 4 players are free, then each adds 2–4%, capped at 300%. The game now literally bills zergs.
- Softcore servers have a raid curfew: raids only 6–9 PM ([Common Ground](https://rust.facepunch.com/news/common-ground)).

**Respawn and death**
- A sleeping bag is a respawn point "with a long cooldown" ([wiki](https://wiki.facepunch.com/rust/item/sleepingbag)). [Guides](https://xgamingserver.com/blog/rust-clans-teams-guide/) put it at about 5 min for a bag and 2 min for a bed.
- Each player can have up to 15 bags ([2023](https://rust.facepunch.com/news/bags-to-battles)).
  - "Bag Gifting" defaults to *Anyone*, so strangers can bag you in.
- Downed players *crawl*, can still open doors, and have a 20% base chance to get back up ([blog](https://rust.facepunch.com/blog/wounding-and-voice-props)).
- Corpses become "player remains" sacks that visibly show whether they've been opened. Ransacked ones strew debris ([Bags to Riches](https://rust.facepunch.com/news/bags-to-riches)).
- The menu's "Suicide" button was renamed "Respawn" in 2024 ([Seismic Shift](https://rust.facepunch.com/news/seismicshift)).

**Sleepers**
- Logging off leaves your body asleep where you stood. It can be killed and looted ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/252490/discussions/0/490125103639502412)).

**Raids**
- **Offline raid:** the owners are asleep inside.
- **Online raid:** the owners are awake and shoot back.
- A wrong code-lock guess *electrocutes* you for 5 damage, ramping up with each miss ([wiki](https://wiki.facepunch.com/rust/item/lock.code~diff:566461)).
- The Seismic Sensor detects explosions, with C4 giving the strongest signal ([2024](https://rust.facepunch.com/news/seismicshift)). It can trigger a Rust+ Smart Alarm that pushes to your phone ([Rust+](https://rust.facepunch.com/companion)). The 3 a.m. "you're being raided" buzz is real.

**Wipe**
- Forced on the first Thursday of every month at 19:00 London time. The next one is 5 Nov 2026.
- A laptop in the Missile Silo counts down to it ([wiki](https://wiki.facepunch.com/rust/server-wipe-timer)).
- The usual arc: a Thursday land-rush; armoured bases and M249s by Monday; a Tuesday latecomer gets offline-raided overnight ([2026 guide](https://www.playnews.gg/en/guides/rust-the-2026-starter-guide-wipe-starter-kit-monuments-base-defense-and-the-10-hours-that-decide-your-survival)).

**Talk**
- Voice chat is proximity-based.
- There's a megaphone, and a powered mic stand with "squeaky" and "deep" voice modes ([blog](https://rust.facepunch.com/blog/wounding-and-voice-props)).
- KOS means kill on sight ([glossary](https://www.ludo.guide/guide/rust/the-ultimate-survival-guide/1-14-rust-terminology-lingo)).

## 2. Iconic items (look → quirk)

**Starter gear**
- **Rock:** a pale cream lump with a blood smear on one edge. A 2025 skin, the "Jungle Idol Rock", "foretells death" ([Jungle](https://rust.facepunch.com/news/jungle-update)).
- **Torch:** a dark stick with a rag collar and a yellow-orange flame. It does 5 damage.
- **Stone hatchet / pickaxe:** a grey stone tied to a stick with rag.

**Guns**
- **Eoka [UNRELIABLE]:** a bent wooden stick with a taped pipe barrel.
  - You bash it repeatedly; the wiki says you "hit the Weapon with a Rock". Each strike *might* fire, then reloading takes about 4 s ([wiki](https://wiki.facepunch.com/rust/item/pistol.eoka~diff:567015)).
  - Its in-game text: "A very cheap, very ineffective, and very unreliable pistol" ([wiki](https://wiki.facepunch.com/rust/item/pistol.eoka)).
- **Bow / crossbow:** plain dark wood. A 4-shot Mini Crossbow arrived in 2025.
- **Double-barrel:** two green pipes on a stock wrapped in tape and rag.
- **Revolver:** silver with a bandaged grip. Its description says "A standard eight shot revolver" (sic) ([wiki](https://wiki.facepunch.com/rust/item/pistol.revolver)).
- **Thompson:** dark metal with wooden furniture.
- **Bolt rifle:** long, scoped, stock wrapped in rag.
- **AK-47 (the status symbol):**
  - Black receiver with a yellow smiley sticker, an orange-taped handguard and a silver-taped magazine.
  - The stock is a red shovel-style D-handle bound in blue tape.
  - Crafting it needs Workbench 3 plus 50 high-quality metal ([wiki](https://wiki.facepunch.com/rust/item/rifle.ak)).

**Explosives and siege weapons**
- **Beancan grenade [UNRELIABLE][BOOM]:** a rusty tin can with "BANG" scrawled on it and a rope fuse.
  - The fuse is a random 3.5–4 s, and 15% are duds ([wiki](https://wiki.facepunch.com/rust/item/grenade.beancan~diff:566994)).
  - Picking a dud back up gives it a 50% chance to explode.
  - Its in-game text warns "you might lose some fingers."
- **Satchel charge [UNRELIABLE][BOOM]:** a tan canvas brick strapped with leather, with three beancan bottoms and a fuse poking out.
  - Roughly 20% dud ([chart](https://www.gameserverkings.com/knowledge-base/rust/rust-raid-chart/)).
  - Touch a dud and it either returns to your inventory or relights on a shorter fuse: "You better get out of there!" ([wiki](https://wiki.facepunch.com/rust/item/explosive.satchel~diff:566760)).
- **C4 (Timed Explosive Charge) [BOOM][LOUD]:** black-and-white bricks wrapped in black duct tape, with a small circuit board on top.
  - It sticks to a wall or door and goes off after 10 s ([Rustafied](https://www.rustafied.com/updates/2015/2/3/new-update-schedule-and-timed-explosive-charges)).
  - Its beeping is famous enough that fans upload it for voice-chat trolling ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gWc3B0AgJUo)). The beep isn't described in the wiki text.
- **Rocket launcher [BOOM][LOUD]:** a rusty bronze pipe with a bandaged grip. Its description: "Unmatched craftsmanship meets devastating firepower" ([wiki](https://wiki.facepunch.com/rust/item/rocket.launcher)).
- **Mortar (2026) [SLOW]:** there's no aiming guide, so you squint through binoculars, and the recoil makes it hop ([update](https://rust.facepunch.com/news/upgrade-hard-raid-harder)).
- **Catapult (2025):** "questionable accuracy". It can launch *players*, and landing is guaranteed death ([Primitive](https://rust.facepunch.com/news/primitive)).

**Building tools and base furniture**
- **Hammer:** a chunky pale wooden mallet.
- **Building Plan:** a blue blueprint sheet.
- **Code lock:**
  - A green metal box with a red LED reading "LOCKED", a cream keypad and an exposed battery.
  - The light is red when locked and green when unlocked ([guide](https://gamever.io/knowledge-base/how-to-make-a-code-lock-in-rust)).
  - Lists of "most-guessed" codes start with 1234, 0000, 1111, 6969 and 1337. These lists are anecdotal ([tradeit](https://tradeit.gg/blog/?p=19693)).
- **Key lock:** a round steel knob. Its text says "don't let it fall in the wrong hands", and the key can be looted off your corpse.
- **Doors:**
  - **Wooden:** pale planks with a Z-brace; weak to fire.
  - **Sheet metal:** a riveted patchwork of rusty plates with blue and orange corrugated strips.
  - **Garage:** a roll-up shutter.
  - **Armoured:** dark riveted steel "with a hatch to see and shoot out of" ([wiki](https://wiki.facepunch.com/rust/item/door.hinged.toptier)).
- **Furnace:** a clay beehive kiln on a stone ring with a glowing mouth.
- **Large furnace:** a tall stacked chimney that "must be placed on terrain". In practice it stands outside like a piñata.
- **Recycler:** comes in three colours.
  - Green at monuments.
  - Yellow in safe zones: weaker and slower, "the price you now must pay for safety" ([2024](https://rust.facepunch.com/news/meta-madness)).
  - Red at the Power Plant: 75% yield, but only when the plant is at full power ([Power Trip](https://rust.facepunch.com/news/power-trip)).
- **Research table:** a drafting table with a blueprint on it. It consumes scrap.
- **Workbenches 1 / 2 / 3:** a wooden shelf → a dark tool bench → a green industrial desk. In 2026, nine rare upgrades were added that visibly bolt onto the bench.
- **Tool Cupboard:** a brown wardrobe with paper notes taped on. Its description: "You should protect this."
- **Sleeping bag:** a patched, drab quilted sack.

**Clothing and armour**
- **The "geared" look:** the standard top kit is a metal facemask, a metal chest plate and a road-sign kilt ([TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/rust-best-pieces-armor/)).
  - **Metal facemask:** a leather cap with a flat welded plate and two rectangular eye slits.
  - **Chest plate:** a steel slab on leather straps.
  - **Road-sign kilt and jacket:** built from a STOP sign (with bullet holes and "KEK" graffiti), a "DANGER DO NOT ENTER" sign, a yellow arrow and a blue parking sign. The item's own description calls it "shoddy".
- **Hazmat suit:** mustard-yellow rubber, a red hood and a black gas mask.
- **Wood armour:** faded green planks roped together.
- **Burlap:** tan sackcloth, with a headwrap that looks like mummy bandages.
- **Boonie hat:** grey-green.
- **Coffee-can helmet:** a red can.
- **Bucket helmet:** a blue bucket with a mesh eyehole.

**Power tools**
- **Jackhammer** (red and black): "requires no aiming… Works well on human flesh" ([wiki](https://wiki.facepunch.com/rust/item/jackhammer)).
- **Chainsaw:** cuts "soft fleshy objects" and runs on low-grade fuel.

**Rides**
- **Horses:** they drop Horse Dung, described as "Fresh and fertile" ([wiki](https://wiki.facepunch.com/rust/item/horsedung)). Since 2025 they ragdoll their riders.
- **Minicopter:** a skeletal rusty frame on three tiny wheels, with an open seat and a two-blade rotor. It costs 750 scrap at Bandit Camp ([devblog](https://rust.double11.com/news/dev-blog-40)).
- **Motorbikes (2024):** one version has a sidecar for a gunner.
- **Bicycles (2024):** riders stand up on the pedals to sprint ([Road Renegades](https://rust.facepunch.com/news/road-renegades)).
- **Player-built boats (Feb 2026):** you can add sails, cannons, a "loud, thirsty" engine and a Plank for pushing friends into the water ([Naval](https://rust.facepunch.com/news/naval)).
- **Fishing:** a steering mini-game. Reel too hard and the line snaps ([wiki](https://wiki.facepunch.com/rust/Fishing)).

**Comic props**
- **Tin Can Alarm:** makes a "loud clatter". The 2026 version can be rigged with grenades or firecrackers.
- **Handcuffs and Prisoner Hood:** the handcuff text reads "You're under arrest!"
- **Homemade Landmine:** "ask a friend to disarm it before it explodes" ([wiki](https://wiki.facepunch.com/rust/item/trap.landmine)).
- **Supply signal:** a grey can with a purple band.

## 3. Monuments and places

**Outpost**
- A walled safe-zone market with vending machines, a drone shop and a recycler.
- Sentry turrets shred anyone flagged hostile ([wiki](https://wiki.facepunch.com/rust/Outpost)). You get flagged by drawing a gun inside, or by shooting someone outside and coming back.
- An olive scientist suit called "Peacekeeper" exists among the item icons. That suggests these are the Outpost guards, but it's an inference.

**Bandit Camp**
- A foggy swamp with wooden shacks, and a casino inside a dredge ([devblog](https://rust.facepunch.com/news/bandit-town-update)).
- **Big Wheel:** you bet scrap on 1, 3, 5, 10 or 20. It spins every 45 s, pays your stake × the number plus your stake back, and the house has the edge.
- There are also slot machines and poker tables, which cost 100 scrap to sit at ([PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/rust/april-update-poker-gestures)).
- The Airwolf vendor sells a minicopter (750 scrap), an attack heli (2,250) and a hot-air balloon (150) ([guide](https://www.bisecthosting.com/blog/rust-bandit-camp-npcs-vendors-missions-casino-roulette)).
- Guards wear heavy armour, black caps and red bandanas.
- Blackjack lives elsewhere, on a casino train car called the Lucky Caboose ([CH](https://www.corrosionhour.com/how-to-play-blackjack-in-rust/)).

**Launch Site**
- A sprawling rocket factory with heavy radiation, about 500 rads, so you need a hazmat suit. A Bradley tank patrols it ([guide](https://supercraft.host/wiki/rust/monument-guide)).
- New in 2026: you can hijack a satellite and steer its crash with thrusters, but "the manual is missing" ([Power Trip](https://rust.facepunch.com/news/power-trip)).

**Oil Rigs (Small, nicknamed "Smoil", and Large)**
- Offshore towers full of scientists.
- Hacking the locked crate sets off sirens, and a Chinook lands heavy scientists ([Ginx](https://www.ginx.tv/en/oil-rig)).

**Cargo Ship** ([Waves of Change](https://rust.facepunch.com/news/waves-of-change))
- Since 2024 it docks at a harbor, where cranes swing containers aboard.
- You can hide inside a container to smuggle yourself on.
- The horn blows 60 s before departure. Miss it and "you might end up back out at sea."

**Military Tunnels**
- A concrete maze with 10–15 scientists. Since Nov 2024 they wear night-vision goggles.

**Airfield, Train Yard and Sewer Branch** ([eip.gg](https://eip.gg/rust/guides/ultimate-rust-monument-guide-loot-callouts-strategy-and-more/))
- **Airfield:** two runways, three hangars and watchtowers.
- **Train Yard:** train cars and gantries; "all rails lead to Train Yard."
- **Sewer Branch:** an easy puzzle and a parkour run.

**The Dome**
- A "Red Giant" sphere tank flanked by red fuel towers, with military crates on top.
- The climb is all pipes and catwalks, and one gap jump kills you if you mistime it ([guide](https://lone.design/rust-sphere-tank-guide-tips-rust-dome-guide/)).
- Since 2026 players can pump it full of crude oil.

**Harbor, Fishing Village and Lighthouse**
- **Harbor:** concrete piers, rusted boats and cranes.
- **Fishing Village:** a wooden dock on stilts. It's a safe zone that sells boats and submarines.
- **Lighthouse:** a lone tower just offshore and a quiet starter spot.

**Junkyard**
- A rust-red heap with a magnet crane you can drive and a car shredder.
- The crane takes heavy damage and is quickly destroyed if you drive it out of the yard ([Rustafied](https://www.rustafied.com/updates/2021/5/6/hdrp-art-and-car-scrapping)).

**Caves**
- Dark, cramped ambush spots (unverified).

**Roads**
- Lined with loot barrels: rusty tan ones, blue biohazard ones, and red oil barrels that drop crude ([wiki](https://wiki.facepunch.com/rust/item/lowgradefuel)).
- Also: road signs, and a Travelling Vendor since 2024 that "can't wait on you forever!" Bradleys can also spawn on roads.

**Newer places**
- **Radtown (2024 remake):** its radioactive water kills plants instantly ([World 2.0](https://rust.facepunch.com/news/world_update_2)).
- **Jungle Ziggurat (2025).**
- **Deep Sea (2026):** floating cities with a casino and a pool, plus ghost ships.
- **Apartment Complex (Jul 2026):** you feed scrap into the slot by your door or get evicted and lose your stuff. A hidden master key opens any room for 5 minutes.

## 4. NPCs, animals and events

**Scientists**
- **Standard:** cobalt-blue hazmat with a gas mask and a "COBALT" patch.
- **Arctic:** white camo.
- **Night-vision:** black, with glowing green goggles.
- **Heavy:** bulky green bomb-suit armour, carrying miniguns that take 1.5 s to spin up ([2024](https://rust.facepunch.com/news/waves-of-change)).
- **Naval (2026):** square visors. They flank, throw smoke and dive into cover.

**Animals**
- The regulars: bears and polar bears, boars, stags, sharks and horses.
- **Wolves:** new AI in 2024 ([update](https://rust.facepunch.com/news/armed-and-animated)).
- **Pet chickens:** need Hunger, Thirst, Sunlight and *Love*, which means head pats ([Crafting](https://rust.facepunch.com/news/crafting-update)).
- **Bees:** break a hive and "the Bees will come."
- **Jungle (2025):**
  - Tigers stalk you from behind, with three warning hints.
  - Crocodiles lurk in murky water.
  - Snakes hiss before striking; you can jump to dodge.
- **Livestock (Oct 2026)** ([Livestock](https://rust.facepunch.com/news/livestock)):
  - Cows and sheep give dung, milk and wool, and can be bred toward a "god clone".
  - The Stable vendor refuses to buy for 15 minutes if you turn down his offer twice.
  - A Biofuel Generator has to be stirred by hand about every 30 minutes.
  - There are also frogs, rabbits, squirrels, turtles, crabs and jellyfish.

**Patrol Helicopter**
- Tours monuments with guns, rocket strafing runs and napalm.
- It ignores naked players. Community guides say it targets anyone wearing about 3+ pieces of clothing or with a gun in their hotbar; the exact threshold is disputed ([CH](https://www.corrosionhour.com/patrol-helicopter-aggression-in-rust/)).
- 2024 added an "orbit and rocket you" phase.
- Since 2025, when it flees it heads to a monument, and that destination is shown on the map ([2025](https://rust.facepunch.com/news/friends-with-benefits)).
- It crashes into burning wreckage, and its crates have to cool before anyone can loot them ([guide](https://xgamingserver.com/blog/rust-bradley-patrol-helicopter-guide/)).

**Chinook**
- Drops a hackable locked crate at a monument. The hack takes 15 minutes, and the whole server can see it on the map.
- Guides openly advise letting someone else start the hack, then killing them ([GamerEmpire](https://gamerempire.net/rust-where-to-get-an-ak/)).

**Bradley APC**
- Has a cannon and a machine gun, and shoots anyone "regardless of what you are wearing".
- Since 2024 it can unload scientists to defend it.

**Airdrop**
- The supply signal gives off "Purple signal smoke" ([wiki](https://wiki.facepunch.com/rust/item/supply.signal)).
- A cargo plane comes over, a crate drops on a parachute, and everyone stampedes toward it.

**Halloween** ([2024](https://rust.facepunch.com/news/halloween24), [Rustafied](https://rustafied.com/updates/2024/10/24/spooky-season-is-here))
- Scarecrows with chainsaws and sickles, plus mummies.
- A 3-minute candy hunt.
- Rift portals that lead to dungeons.
- 2024 added a Frankenstein table for stitching together a pet monster.

**Christmas**
- A jingle plays, presents spawn near every player, and you whack them open.
- Santa's sleigh drops a giant gift ([Rustafied](https://www.rustafied.com/updates/2020/12/17/rust-christmas-starts-today)).
- Stockings fill up when the bells ring, with "no coal here!" ([2025](https://rust.facepunch.com/news/merry-rustmas-25)).
- **Looks:**
  - Small present: purple with a pink ribbon.
  - Large present: red with gold stars.
  - Stocking: green, with a red cuff and a bell on the toe.
  - Candy-cane club.

## 5. Player archetypes and tropes

- **The naked / freshie:** carries only a rock and travels in packs. Even Facepunch's own medieval door skin keeps out "~~peasants~~ nakeds" ([Primitive](https://rust.facepunch.com/news/primitive)).
- **The geared chad:** facemask, chest plate, kilt and an AK.
- **Solo vs zerg:** a zerg is 6–8 or more players ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/252490/discussions/0/1728701877515114973/)), and is now taxed on upkeep.
- **Roof camper and door camper:** sits on their own roof with a gun, or waits outside your door for you to come out ([YT](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uFw5OabsfvA), [YT](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W8aQKDzASnY)).
- **The rat:** waits for heli crates to cool, or for you to finish a hack.
- **The builder who never raids:** 47 airlocks, honeycomb everywhere, wallpaper (2024).
- **The gambler:** lives at the Big Wheel.
- **The "friendly?" betrayer:**
  - The canonical clip is SovietWomble's (2016). He killed a man, then stripped naked to pose as a newbie.
  - The man's friends gave him supplies, and he shot them with the dead man's rifle, set to Enya's "Only Time" ([Player.One](https://www.player.one/rust-gameplay-showcases-most-wicked-devious-example-online-betrayal-sovietwomble-504423)).
- **The trader:** sells from Outpost vending machines. Since 2026 there are rentable shops, which rivals can take over after 6 hours by paying double rent.
- **The kidnapper / role-player:** uses cuffs and a hood. Back in 2014, role-play towns had a sheriff's jail and naked revolver duels ([PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/rust-not-quite-full-bastards-you-might-expect)).
- **Screaming kid / dad on mic:** in one viral raid, the victim cried, offered to settle it with rock-paper-scissors and threatened to report the raider ([Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/esports/news-brutal-rust-raid-leaves-player-tears-threatens-ban-avail)). Rock-paper-scissors became an official gesture mini-game in 2024.
- **Bag-in strangers.**
- **The insider:** a teammate with your codes and TC access who walks off with everything.
- **The trap / honeypot builder:**
  - Shotgun traps behind double doors, hidden auto turrets, flame turrets (a white tank with orange face graffiti), collapsing floors and Russian-roulette cells ([PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/rust/trap-base)).
  - ZChum's "Blood Club" (Nov 2025) promised a whole server a dance party with bbno$, then dropped the floor and blew it up ([PC Gamer via inkl](https://www.inkl.com/news/this-rust-blood-club-lures-an-entire-server-inside-before-pulling-the-literal-floor-out-from-under-the-party-and-exploding)).
- **The musician:** plays the Wheelbarrow Piano, Jerry Can Guitar or Xylobones ([2019](https://rust.facepunch.com/news/instrument-update)).
- **The farmer:** chicken whisperer or beekeeper.

## 6. Memes, catchphrases and viral moments

**Established**
- **"Hello?… Friendly? Don't shoot!"** is the universal opener.
  - A PC Gamer diarist was circled by a stranger who kept saying in a monotone that he was friendly and that it was fine to open the door ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/au/rust-diary-part-2-its-hard-to-kill-a-half-naked-jumping-man)).
  - Built-in irony: doing the official "friendly" (surrender) gesture is exactly what lets someone handcuff you ([2024](https://rust.facepunch.com/news/road-renegades)).
- **KOS, "offline raided", "door camped" and "roofed"** are all everyday terms.
- **The rock as the deadliest weapon:** its icon is literally bloodstained.
- **Beancan duds and eoka misfires:** the official item descriptions already tell the joke.
- **"Wipe day":** the first-Thursday apocalypse, with a doomsday laptop counting down to it.
- **"Built a 2x2, raided in 10 minutes":** the 2x2 is the default beginner base, and twig and wood bases are "raided in seconds" ([guide](https://xgamingserver.com/blog/rust-beginners-guide-wipe-day/)).
- **"Code 1234."**
- **C4 soundboard trolling:** playing the C4 beep over voice chat to make people panic.

**Unverified**
- **"Rust moment":** used as general shorthand for absurd deaths.
- **"You have no door":** I couldn't find it as an established meme. The underlying gag of a newbie with an unlocked door is real.
- **"Bush-wookie":** generic survival-shooter slang, not specific to Rust.

**Viral and press moments**
- Markiplier's 2013 Rust video, with 3.7M+ views ([KYM](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/subcultures/rust)).

**Signature sounds**
- **Tools:** a "thock" on trees, a "tink" on ore, and the crack of a falling tree.
- **Doors:** door slams, a rattling garage door, a heavy clank for armoured doors.
- **Code lock:** keypad beeps; a wrong code earns a buzz and a zap.
- **Explosives:**
  - C4: beep-beep-boom.
  - Beancan: a fizzing fuse, then silence if it's a dud.
  - Eoka: scrape-scrape-click-BANG.
- **Events:**
  - Helicopter: rotor thump and rocket whoosh.
  - Cargo ship horn and oil-rig sirens.
  - Tin-can clatter.
  - Sleigh bells.
- **Footsteps:** they sound different on wood, stone and metal ([ggservers](https://ggservers.com/blog/10-deep-curiosities-and-hidden-mechanics-of-rust/)).
- **Voices:** the mic stand's squeaky and deep voice modes.
- **Muffling:** since 2026 the Heavy Plate Helmet muffles all game audio.
- **Caveats:**
  - These descriptions of how things sound come from play, not documentation.
  - I found nothing to confirm an upgrade "boop". Upgrades seem to play building sounds specific to the material (unverified).
