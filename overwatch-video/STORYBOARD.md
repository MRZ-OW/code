# Support Main: storyboard

**Logline:** Clawd wants to be a good support, but its Overwatch team keeps spamming "I need healing" and then gets wiped by a High Noon, so Clawd rises with "Heroes never die", brings everyone back and earns Play of the Game, only for Genji to ask for healing one more time.

**Length / tempo:** 50 s at 120 BPM (beat 0.5 s, bar 2 s). Every cut lands on a beat; big changes on bar lines.

**World:** a western payload map (desert main street, mesas, a saloon, the payload on rails) bracketed by the team's spawn room.
Palette arc: cool spawn-room teal → bright turquoise noon street → burning orange **High Noon** → gold **Valkyrie** light → sunset victory → the same spawn room, warm.

**Cast** (all Clawds in hero costume, team = blue-ish rim, enemy = red):
| who | costume | role |
|---|---|---|
| **Mercy-Clawd** | gold winged headpiece, white wings, Caduceus staff, yellow heal beam | our hero, the support main |
| **Rein-Clawd** | steel crested helmet, rocket hammer, blue hex barrier | tank, charges ahead |
| **Tracer-Clawd** | spiky hair, orange goggles, glowing blue chronal disc, blink streaks | DPS, never stands still |
| **Genji-Clawd** | silver faceplate with green visor slit, green sword glow | DPS, **always** needs healing |
| **Cowboy** (enemy) | wide hat, red serape, revolver, cigar | the High Noon |

**Motif:** the painted **"+" healing call** (a white plus on a red badge). It pops over heads all film long; at the end the team pops **hearts** instead (payoff) — and then Genji pops one last "+".

**Clawd's arc:** happy → overwhelmed (surprised, nervous) → annoyed → scared → sad → determined → starstruck/proud → love → deadpan.

**Text:** none except one painted banner, **PLAY OF THE GAME**, used once (the joke needs it). No signs, no labels, no speech.

## Checks
- Event in every shot, cause then reaction, transitions at every seam (iris, whip, tilt, push, whip, banner, banner, iris).
- One read at a time in the timing column; the big moments (gate, High Noon bangs, wings, POTG) get holds.
- Only one painted text, used once. Ending rhymes with the opening (same spawn room, hearts instead of "+").
